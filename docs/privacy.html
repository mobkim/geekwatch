Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 31, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains what data the geekwatch Discord bot ("geekwatch", "the Bot") collects and how it is used.

1. Data We Collect

To provide follow/watch notifications, geekwatch stores:

  • Your Discord user ID (when you follow a topic or board, or opt in to DM notifications)
  • Discord server (guild) IDs and channel IDs where notifications are configured
  • The geekhack.org topics and boards you choose to follow or watch (topic/board IDs, titles, and URLs)
  • Channel restriction settings configured by server admins

We do not collect message content from your Discord server, geekhack account credentials, email addresses, or any other personal information beyond the identifiers above.

2. How We Use This Data

Data is used solely to determine where and to whom notifications about new geekhack topics or replies should be sent, and to let you manage your own follow list via the Bot's commands.

3. Data Storage

Data is stored in a database operated by the Bot's maintainer. It is retained for as long as your follow/watch preferences remain active, or until you remove them or the Bot is removed from your server.

4. Data Sharing

We do not sell or share your data with third parties. Data is not used for advertising or analytics.

5. Data Deletion

You can remove your own data at any time using the Bot's `/unfollow all` command (or the DM opt-out), which deletes your stored follow list. Server admins can remove all trackers within a channel using the Bot's mass-removal command. You may also request full deletion of your data by contacting us via the link below.

6. Third-Party Services

geekwatch interacts with Discord (via Discord's API) and reads publicly available pages on geekhack.org. Your use of Discord and geekhack.org is separately governed by their own privacy policies.

7. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Continued use of the Bot after changes take effect constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.

8. Contact

Questions or data deletion requests can be raised via the GitHub repository.
